# Banking/KYC visual review — 27 October 2026 (India)

`before/` captures the 0.11 production output before rebuilding: Learn, About and S0.4.2 as an outline. `after/` captures the same routes plus the actual first reading and initial board in 0.12. `board/` captures fictional request inspection, payment-route comparison and hold/debit reconciliation. Matrix: 360×800 / 1440×900, English/Hindi, light/dark, reduced motion. Contact sheets supplement individual viewport screenshots. These are browser emulations, not physical-device photos.

## Observations and limits

- The topic now offers a draft lesson while the original bank-statement lesson remains independently accessible. The original catalogue topic title is still English in Hindi mode; its localisation backlog is explicit.
- Request and payment panels show one explanation at a time. Panel controls wrap into full-width phone buttons and fit across desktop rows. Text, pressed borders and visible keyboard focus accompany colour.
- The reconciliation panel shows the payment amount beside its slider, followed by the recorded and assumed-available balances. Pending/posting switches expose the remaining hold and extra debit separately, so the same payment is not silently counted twice.
- The long Hindi/English lesson title wraps on phones. Reading and expanded practice panels need vertical scrolling. Both balance cards and all actions do not fit simultaneously in an 800px viewport; the labels and nearby output remain readable while scrolling. Nested surfaces/borders are visually dense and need learner review before claiming a final design.
- Automated checks cover 44px buttons, keyboard activation and live range updates, 200% text without document/dialog horizontal overflow, and whole-page axe scans for all three panels in all eight combinations. Screenshots were inspected at full phone/desktop sizes and via contact sheets.
- The fictional examples cannot establish a current document list, approve an account or guarantee a real bank’s available balance. Financial/native-language review, TalkBack, physical touch, low-end-device smoothness and production deployment remain unverified.
