## Observations and limits

- The topic now links to an authored draft. Original EMI and purchase-credit lessons remain open. The original catalogue title is still English in Hindi mode as part of the documented outline-localisation backlog.
- Panel and classification controls wrap into full-width phone buttons; desktop panels fit across a row. Text/pressed state and keyboard focus accompany colour. A purpose-only advertisement can be classified as insufficient information, without guessing its security.
- Cost controls show the deducted fee and repayment period beside their sliders. Cash received appears immediately after the fee slider. The stress panel repeats the new monthly payment beside its control area, so live changes do not depend on finding a distant summary.
- English and Hindi are legible in both themes. Large titles and long assumptions wrap on phones. Four summary cards stack vertically; the whole panel does not fit in 800px. Some details/reset controls remain below the fold. Nested surfaces are dense and need learner review before calling the design final.
- Automated checks cover 44px targets, keyboard activation/slider changes, 200% text without document/dialog horizontal overflow and whole-page axe scans across all three panels in eight combinations. They do not replace human accessibility, TalkBack, low-end touch or performance testing.
- The rupee totals are explicitly rounded displays of unrounded calculations. Current legal/lender rules, full APR, product suitability and predicted future rates are not certified by these fictional cases. Financial/native-language and physical-device review remain unverified.
