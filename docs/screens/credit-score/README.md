# Credit-report visual review — 23 October 2026

`before/` records the existing 0.10 production output before rebuilding: Learn, About and the S0.6.5 outline. `after/` records the same routes plus the real first teaching card and initial report board in 0.11. `board/` records report-error inspection, the starting ratio and action comparisons. Matrix: 360×800 / 1440×900, English/Hindi, light/dark, reduced motion. Contact sheets aid comparison; individual PNGs retain viewport dimensions. This is browser evidence, not physical-device validation.

## Observations and limits

- The former outline now links to an authored draft. The original English catalogue heading remains in Hindi mode as part of the documented localisation backlog.
- Four report-entry buttons expose one explanation at a time. Three panel controls wrap into full-width phone buttons; desktop controls fit across a row. Text and pressed/focus borders accompany colour.
- The ratio panel shows the outstanding balance separately from the percentage. Controls show their current rupee values; the calculation explains unknown limits and above-limit values. No score gauge, approval band or predicted point gain is presented.
- English and Hindi remain legible in both themes. Native sliders and buttons have 44px targets; keyboard activation/arrow adjustments and 200% text reflow are tested. Whole-page axe scans cover all three panels in all eight combinations.
- Phone panels still require vertical scrolling, especially the ratio panel. The formula and preset buttons can be below the fold; current amounts remain beside their slider labels. Nested cards/borders create a dense appearance. Learner studies should assess passage length and control placement before calling the design final.
- Evidence uses fictional records only. Automated checks do not establish financial accuracy, natural native-language tone, TalkBack usability, smooth low-end touch or real-device performance.
