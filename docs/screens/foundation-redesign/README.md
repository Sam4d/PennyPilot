# UX evidence

Before: the v0.7 screens. `tokens`: the design-token migration, captured at 360x800 and 1440x900 in English/Hindi and light/dark themes. Fixtures contain no personal data. Screenshots are evidence of appearance, not device performance or editorial approval.

Initial critique: the original hierarchy is pleasant but fine print and Devanagari spacing are small, Hindi screens contain English UI copy, and mobile navigation labels are cramped. The token pass increases contrast, input/touch dimensions and help-text readability without replacing Mia or the white/charcoal/green identity. It does not by itself complete onboarding, localisation or the curriculum. Source/model content often needs vertical scrolling; this is preferable to shrinking explanatory text.

Validation for the token pass: content validation, 59 unit checks (including semantic colour-pair contrast), build and the full 65-case production browser suite pass. Two development-server checks were initially invalidated by hot reload during migration; the subsequent production suite uses a settled built artifact. No failed check is counted as a pass.

## Foundation redesign review

`after/` contains 104 review captures: 13 states in eight configurations (360×800 and 1440×900, English/Hindi, light/dark). States cover onboarding, home, revision, calculators, insurance, notebook, profile, About, curriculum, lesson introduction, exercise, feedback and exit confirmation. The 48 files in `visual-baselines/` cover the six main screens across the same matrix and are used by Playwright. Fixtures use fresh local progress, not invented active learners.

Visual review: Mia, restrained green accents and the learning path remain original. The new introduction gives language selection and a single start action a clearer hierarchy. Draft status is visible before teaching; feedback uses symbols and words alongside colour. Hindi paragraphs have more breathing room, and dark surfaces preserve hierarchy. Review caught the phone navigation wrapping words and the home introduction extending into Mia's illustration; removing excess navigation padding and reserving paragraph space corrected them. It also caught a notebook placeholder bypassing translation, which was corrected. The final phone home and Hindi notebook captures were inspected after these fixes.

Limits: secondary copy and six bottom-navigation labels are still compact on a 360 px phone. English onboarding's final explanatory notice can require scrolling at 800 px height; its primary action is visible. Source lists, worked examples, insurance articles and About copy also require scrolling. Desktop cards are deliberately spacious but some explanatory areas remain dense. These screenshots do not prove fluent Hindi, physical-device animation smoothness, TalkBack usability, or undergraduate learning outcomes. The large English planning catalogue still requires multilingual authoring. Four-pass teaching boards and cumulative capstone assessment are proposed, not implemented by this UI patch.

The initial measured layout shift and all final check results are recorded in [VALIDATION.md](../../VALIDATION.md). Local Chrome throttling is not a Lighthouse score or a real low-memory Android measurement. No software licence has been selected.

Final verification: content validation, 62 unit tests, build, all 78 production browser cases and a separate unchanged-baseline comparison of 48 screenshots pass. Offline and actual service-worker replacement checks pass. Three isolated throttled local samples measured LCP 1.760–1.780 seconds, first-question readiness 4.840–4.867 seconds (including observation waits), and startup CLS 0.0225; no additional question-open shift was observed. These are local measured timings, not Lighthouse scores or real-device guarantees. Hindi desktop breadcrumbs, catalogue status badges and learning-node accessible status labels were corrected after review; the final catalogue capture was inspected.
